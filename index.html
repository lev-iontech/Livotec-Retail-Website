How every glass is made

Water passes through pre-filtration, the RO membrane and functional post-filters before it reaches your tap. PureLite and FlexTemp use 10 stages. VitaGlow uses 11 stages with added minerals.

  1. 1

    Pre-filter 1

    Removes sand, moss and sediment.

  2. 2

    Pre-filter 2

    Removes chlorine and organic molecules.

  3. 3

    Pre-filter 3

    Removes remaining residues.

  4. RO

    RO membrane

    99.99% removal of bacteria, viruses, heavy metals and arsenic.

  5. +

    Post-filters

    T33-GAC for taste, minerals, ORP alkaline and Hydrogen Plus, depending on model.

What your home needs for free standard installation

Check these before your survey. All electrical, plumbing and drain provisions must be ready before the unit can be installed.

  • Free 2 service visits. 3rd visit onwards is subject to charges.
  • Water supply within 1.5 meters
  • Water drain within 3 meters
  • Electrical outlet within 1 meter
  • Maynilad / Manila Water supply
  • Elevator access or ≤3 floors
  • Non-marble / granite sink
  • Simple sink or countertop modification

If the water source or drain is more than 3 meters away, or the electrical supply more than 1 meter away, additional charges for labor and materials apply.
